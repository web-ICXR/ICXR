Call for Proposals "XR Gallery"

Everywhere, Everywhen: Immersive Experiences that Bring Us Together

Overview

Immersive media offer a unique ability to transcend the constraints of space and time, connecting people, places, and moments through shared experiences. This year, we invite submissions exploring how extended reality and immersive art foster connection beyond the here and now. We welcome works across the full spectrum of XR—including augmented (AR), mixed (MR), and virtual reality (VR)—using any creative approach, from generative and intelligent systems to handcrafted experiences. The call is open to artists, researchers, designers, technologists, and all creators of immersive experiences.

Topics and Scope

As part of ICXR 2026, held on November 5–6, 2026 in Guangzhou, China — a dynamic innovation hub in the Greater Bay Area — we invite submissions to the second XR Gallery, titled Everywhere, Everywhen: Immersive Experiences that Bring Us Together. We aim to provoke thought and discussion between artists and researchers around the question of “How Can Immersive Experiences Bring Us Together Beyond Space and Time?”. By bringing together the XR, technology, and art communities, we hope to inspire new perspectives on connection across places, moments, and communities.

The main requirements for the submissions are as follows:

  • Interactive XR artworks, installations, technologies, and experiences are encouraged, including single-user and multi-user, collaborative, participatory, and storytelling experiences. Other digital media and creative works aligned with the exhibition theme are also welcome.
  • Properly defined space and equipment required
  • At least one presenter presents at the conference
  • The proposed artistic work may be new or already existing

Accepted submissions will be exhibited in the dedicated XR Gallery space throughout the ICXR conference.

Submission

This year, the XR Gallery offers Two Submission Types. Please select the one that best fits your work when submitting through the conference submission system (PCS): https://new.precisionconference.com/icxr26b.

  • Full Paper: Minimum 8 pages of main text, excluding references and appendix. Accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings (Springer LNCS, indexed by EI Compendex).
  • Artwork Description: 2 pages. Accepted submissions will not be published in the conference proceedings but will be exhibited in the XR Gallery throughout the conference.

Regardless of the chosen type, each submission should include:

  • Paper (Full Paper: minimum 8 pages of main text, excluding references and appendix; Artwork Description: 2 pages), formatted in the LNCS template
  • Description of the art project
  • Implementation details (how it will be executed, e.g., HMD or installation project, etc.)
  • Representative image
  • Video
  • Information on equipment and technology used (ICXR only supports the provision of basic facilities such as table, power, network, display space, etc.)

Any anonymized supplementary material is also welcomed but not required.

Anonymous Submission

All submissions must be anonymous. Please remove author names, affiliations, and acknowledgements from the paper, and avoid any content in the text, images, video, or supplementary material that identifies the authors or their institutions.

Paper Format

All submissions must use the LNCS one-column page format. Full Papers are a minimum of 8 pages of main text, excluding references and appendix. Artwork Descriptions are 2 pages.

Selection Process

Each submission will be examined by a panel of external assessors from the digital arts fields and the XR research communities, based on the following criteria:

  • Originality
  • How well it fits in the scope of the XR Gallery and this year’s theme
  • How compellingly it brings people together across space and time
  • Representative video and image
  • The equipment and technical information required for its realization (including light, sound, network needs)
