Call for Labs Spotlight Proposals

Overview

We invite research groups from all areas of XR (Augmented, Mixed, and Virtual Reality), 3D User Interfaces, and related fields to apply to present in the “Labs Spotlight” session at the International Conference on Extended Reality 2026 (ICXR 2026). This premier session is designed to provide a high-visibility stage for academic and industrial research groups worldwide to showcase their cutting-edge work, state-of-the-art facilities, and future research visions in the field of Extended Reality (XR).

Presentations may highlight the group’s research areas, team members, facilities, ongoing and past projects, academic and industrial collaborations, and open opportunities, welcoming participation from both academic and industrial labs. These presentations aim to attract top talent by pitching open positions such as Ph.D., postdoc, and internships directly to an audience of highly motivated students and young researchers, while also boosting visibility by putting a spotlight on the group’s achievements and unique capabilities in front of leading experts and industry pioneers. In addition, they foster collaborations by connecting with potential academic partners and industry sponsors to spark new collaborative projects.

While presentation materials will not appear in the official ICXR proceedings, a list of participating labs and their contact details will be featured on the ICXR 2026 website, providing lasting visibility to the broader XR community.

活动介绍

第三届国际扩展现实会议（ICXR 2026）由中国计算机学会（CCF）主办，致力于推进虚拟现实、增强现实、混合现实及人工智能相关技术研究与创新。该会议入选了CCF高质量国际学术会议培育计划，以及CCF国际学术会议推荐列表。

我们诚邀来自扩展现实（增强现实、混合现实、虚拟现实）、三维用户界面及相关领域的各个研究团队，申请参加将于2026年举办的国际扩展现实大会（ICXR 2026）上的“实验室聚焦（Labs Spotlight）”环节。这一顶级环节旨在为全球学术界和工业界的研究团队提供一个高知名度的舞台，展示他们在扩展现实领域的前沿工作、先进设施以及未来研究愿景。

展示内容可涵盖实验室的研究方向、团队成员、设施设备、正在进行及已完成的项目、学术与产业合作，以及开放的合作机会，欢迎学术界和工业界的实验室参与。这些展示旨在通过直接向积极进取的学生和青年研究人员推介博士、博士后、实习等开放职位来吸引顶尖人才；同时通过在领先专家和行业先驱面前突出团队的成就与独特能力来提升知名度。此外，还能通过与潜在学术伙伴和行业赞助商建立联系，激发新的合作项目。

虽然展示材料不会出现在ICXR 2026的正式会议论文集中，但参与实验室的名单及其联系方式将在ICXR 2026官网上展示，从而为更广泛的XR社区提供持续的可见性。
