Paper Registration Guide

Oral and Poster papers: every accepted Oral or Poster paper requires a paid Paper Registration. A 2-paper ticket can cover two Oral papers, two Poster papers, or one of each; it still includes conference attendance for one attendee only.

Every accepted paper must be covered by a paid Paper Registration. This requirement applies to all papers, regardless of whether the registering author is a student, a professional, a CCF member or a non-member. Full Conference Pass and Student Pass (Banquet Excluded) are attendance-only tickets and do not cover papers.

  1. Paper Registration (1 Paper): covers ONE accepted paper (one Paper ID) and includes conference attendance for ONE attendee.
  2. Paper Registration (2 Papers): covers TWO accepted papers (two different Paper IDs) in a single registration and includes conference attendance for ONE attendee only. The numbers in the ticket names refer to papers, not attendees.
  3. A paper needs paid registration coverage only once. If a coauthor has already registered and paid for the paper, other coauthors do not need to register that same paper again. Each additional coauthor who wishes to attend must register separately and purchase an appropriate attendance ticket.
  4. Students: a student responsible for registering an uncovered paper must choose a Paper Registration ticket, not a student attendance ticket. A student coauthor whose paper is already covered may choose an eligible attendance ticket. CCF membership determines the applicable member/non-member fee; it does not change the number of papers or attendees covered.
  5. Paper IDs: enter only the paper(s) covered by this registration. A 1-paper ticket requires one Paper ID; a 2-paper ticket requires two DISTINCT Paper IDs, separated by a comma (e.g. 123,456). Use the IDs assigned by the submission system.
  6. If your situation is not covered above, or if you have any other questions, please contact icxr@ccf.org.cn before payment to confirm your registration arrangements.

Visa and Invitation Letters

Visa & Invitation Letter

If you need to apply for a Chinese visa, please complete conference registration and payment before requesting an invitation letter. Email icxr@ccf.org.cn with the subject ICXR 2026 Invitation Letter Request – Full Name.

Please include the following in your email

  • Passport details: full name as shown on your passport, gender, date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD), nationality and passport number.
  • Affiliation: institution/company, position and your role at the conference (e.g. author, speaker or attendee).
  • Expenses: the person or organization covering your travel and attendance expenses.
  • Registration details: the email address used for conference registration, and your registration/order number, if available.

After verifying your registration details, we will email you a personalized conference invitation letter in PDF format. One letter is issued per individual attendee. The invitation letter supports your visa application; visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

Special invitation requirements

For applicants from most countries and regions, a standard invitation letter is generally sufficient to support a visa application. If the Chinese embassy, consulate or visa application center handling your application requires an Invitation Letter (TE), an Invitation Verification Letter or other specific documents, and you are an author of a paper accepted by ICXR 2026, please contact icxr@ccf.org.cn. Please specify your passport type and the country and city where you plan to submit your Chinese visa application. Whether these documents can be provided, and the arrangements for obtaining them, must be confirmed separately.

论文注册选票指南

适用范围：每篇录用论文均须办理论文注册，包括 Oral（口头报告）论文和 Poster（海报展示）论文。双篇票可覆盖两篇 Oral、两篇 Poster，或各一篇，仍只包含1名参会者的参会权益。

每篇录用论文都必须由已付费的论文注册票覆盖；无论负责注册的作者是学生、专业人员、CCF会员还是非会员，此要求均适用。普通全日票和学生票（不含晚宴）仅用于参会，不覆盖论文。

  1. 论文注册（1篇）：覆盖1篇录用论文（1个Paper ID），并包含1名参会者的参会权益。
  2. 论文注册（2篇）：通过一次注册覆盖2篇录用论文（2个不同的Paper ID），仍只包含1名参会者的参会权益。票名中的数字指论文篇数，不是参会人数。
  3. 同一篇论文只需付费覆盖一次。如果共同作者已为该论文完成注册和缴费，其他共同作者无须再次为同一篇论文办理论文注册。其他希望参会的共同作者须各自报名并购买适合自己的参会票。
  4. 学生作者：负责为尚未覆盖的论文办理注册时，须选择论文注册票，不能用学生参会票代替。如果论文已由共同作者完成付费覆盖，学生共同作者可按自身资格选择参会票。CCF会员身份决定适用会员价或非会员价，不改变所覆盖的论文篇数和参会人数。
  5. Paper ID：仅填写本次注册实际覆盖的论文编号。单篇票填写1个Paper ID；双篇票填写2个不同的Paper ID，以英文逗号分隔（例如123,456）。请使用投稿系统分配的论文编号。
  6. 如有上述说明未覆盖的情况，或其他疑问，请在缴费前联系 icxr@ccf.org.cn 确认注册安排。

签证与邀请函

如果您需要办理中国签证，请完成会议注册和缴费后，再申请邀请函。请发送邮件至 icxr@ccf.org.cn，邮件主题为 ICXR 2026 Invitation Letter Request – 姓名。

请在邮件中提供

  • 护照信息：与护照一致的姓名、性别、出生日期（YYYY-MM-DD）、国籍及护照号码。
  • 单位信息：所在院校或单位、职务及参会身份（如作者、演讲嘉宾或普通参会者）。
  • 费用来源：承担此次行程和参会费用的个人或单位。
  • 注册信息：会议报名使用的电子邮箱，以及报名／订单编号（如有）。

我们将在核对注册信息后，通过邮件发送个人会议邀请函 PDF。邀请函按实际参会者一人一函出具。邀请函用于支持签证申请，签证审批由有关主管机关决定。

特殊邀请材料要求

对于大多数国家和地区，一般邀请信已足够支持签证申请。如受理申请的中国使领馆或签证申请中心要求提供《邀请函（TE）》《邀请核实单》或其他特定材料，且您是本届ICXR已录用论文的作者，请联系 icxr@ccf.org.cn。请说明您的护照类型，以及您计划在哪个国家、哪个城市提交中国签证申请。此类材料能否办理及具体安排须另行确认。
