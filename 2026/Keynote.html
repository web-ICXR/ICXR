Frank Steinicke

Title: Next-Generation Spatial Platforms at the Intersection of XR and Embodied AI

Abstract: Virtual, Augmented, and Mixed Realities are no longer mere display mechanisms—they represent the perceptual sensory cortex for embodied AI systems. The keynote investigates how combining low-latency spatial tracking and display with real-time AI reasoning models transforms ambient computing into an synergistic collaboration between human and AI.

Bio: Frank Steinicke is a professor of Human-Computer Interaction at the Department of Informatics at the Universität Hamburg. Before his current position, he was a professor of Computer Science in Media at the Department of Computer Science at the University of Würzburg and chair of the Immersive Media Group from 2011 to 2014. He studied Mathematics with a Minor in Computer Science at the University of Münster, from which he received his Ph.D. and Venia Legendi in Computer Science. His research interests are focused on merging natural with artificial intelligence and realities to improve interactions and experiences in computer-mediated environments. For his pioneering work, he received the IEEE VGTC Virtual Reality Technical Achievement Award in 2023 and was inducted into the prestigious IEEE VR Academy. In 2024, he was inaugurated in the Academy of Sciences and Humanities in Hamburg, and was inducted into AWE's XR Hall of Fame in 2025 and into the Scientific Research Honor Society.

Gerard Jounghyun Kim

Title: Usability, "the Final Piece of the Puzzle for Proliferation of the XR", is within Reach!

Abstract: To be announced.

Bio: Gerard Jounghyun Kim received the B.S. degree in electrical and computer engineering from Carnegie Mellon University, in 1987, and the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in computer science from the University of Southern California in 1994. He began his academic career at POSTECH in 1996 and joined Korea University, in 2006 where he is currently a Professor of Computer Science and Engineering. He served as the Dean of the College of Informatics from 2024 to 2026. His research interests include human–computer interaction, virtual and mixed reality, and computer music. Dr. Kim has published over 200 research articles, authored two international books, and served as General and Program Chair for numerous international conferences. He was also the founding convener of the ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 24/WG 9 Working Group on Mixed and Augmented Reality (MAR) Standardization.
